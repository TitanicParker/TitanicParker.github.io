Fiche Ceathar · Current intelligibility framework

The Intelligibility Gallery

How does something become understandable enough to count as achieved—and what remains when it does?

We recognise differences, discover possibilities, establish relationships, repair faults, integrate contributions and recognise when a question has been answered. Fiche Ceathar asks what makes each of these achievements sufficient.

The work begins with three questions: What changed? What made that change sufficient? How does the achievement remain available? Its proposed grammar, The 54, describes fifty-four unified forms through Dimensional Conjunction. No memorisation is needed to begin.

The shelf: an ordinary example

“The shelf holds weight because its parts brace one another.” The achievement is an explanation. Replace because with and, and the topic remains while the explanatory relation disappears. This helps reveal the Operative Centre.

What is established, and what remains open.

The framework defines its fifteen primitives and fifty-four proposed forms and provides extensive illustrations. Universal exhaustiveness and independent classification reliability remain research questions. Curated examples and protocols do not, by themselves, establish experimental success.
